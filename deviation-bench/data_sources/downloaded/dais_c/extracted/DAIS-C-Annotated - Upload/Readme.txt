The Discussion Abstract Ideas in Schizophrenia Corpus (DAIS-C). 

Details:

Full details on the design and construction of the corpus is available here: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666799123000291

Contents:

The folders are organised into clinical (CL) and comparison (CO) subcorpora. 

Within these folders, files are organised into:

- interactional (speaker and interviewer transcripts, running dialogue)
- speaker only raw (each speaker's text with no interviewer speech or XML tags), 
- speaker only for analysis (each speaker's text with no interviewer speech but with XML tags)
- timestamped (speaker and interviewer timestamps, minus speech, running dialogue)

Details of the XML tags are given in the publication mentioned above. Those not mentioned there are:

<Gr>: grammatical features of interest
<WS>: word selection features of interest
<TC>: thought completion features of interest
<DT>: discourse tracking features of interest 

These were part of the authors' own investigation and are included here in the hope that they may be useful to others.

Template informed consent and medical declaration forms are also provided.
